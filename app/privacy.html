Privacy

BAPHY105 Notes is a small study website for VIT students, run by a student. This page explains what it stores about you and why. Last updated 28 September 2026.

What is stored

  • Your VIT student email, from "Sign in with Google". It is used to let you in, to link your payment to your account, and to apply the device limit. Your name and profile photo from Google are not stored.
  • When you first signed in, when you last used the site, and how many times you have signed in, shown to the site owner in a list of students together with your access (paid, free or Module 2) and how many phones and computers you use.
  • A random device code kept in your browser, so the site can tell your phone from your laptop for the "1 phone + 1 laptop/PC" limit.
  • Your payment record: the Razorpay payment ID, amount and date, linked to your email. UPI details are handled entirely by Razorpay; this site never sees your bank or UPI details.
  • Messages you choose to send with Help? or Any ideas?, and device reset requests, together with your student email so the owner can reply.
  • Quiz battles: the two players' emails, the questions and their answers, deleted automatically after 3 hours. Opponents only see each other's first name.
  • Anonymous counts that are not linked to you: daily visitors, how often each topic is opened, how many people get each quiz question right or wrong, which tabs are marked "Confusing?", and how many people reach the payment page.

Your progress (topics done, quiz scores, flashcards, highlights, your own notes, syllabus ticks, study time and streak) is saved in your browser and also stored on the server, linked to your email, so it follows you between your phone and laptop. The owner sees only a summary of it in the list of students: your total and today's study time, your streak and how many topics and modules you have finished, not your notes or highlights. Share cards are made in your browser and only shared if you share them.

What is not done

  • Your data is not sold or shared with anyone, and not used for ads.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies are used. The only cookie keeps you signed in.

Where it is kept

The site runs on Vercel, with a small Upstash database for the records above. Payments go through Razorpay. Google handles sign-in.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the menu. To have your email and payment record deleted, write to notesbugsreportandsupport@gmail.com from your student account. Deleting your record also removes your access.

Questions or problems: notesbugsreportandsupport@gmail.com.
